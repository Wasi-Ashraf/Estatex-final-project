## 1. Executive Summary

EstateX is a comprehensive real estate discovery and listing management web platform designed specifically for the Pakistani property market. It addresses fundamental inefficiencies in the local property sector — fragmented classifieds, non-standardized property measurements, opaque pricing, and disorganized buyer-seller communication channels.

EstateX establishes a verified, structured platform where:
- **Visitors and Buyers** can search, filter, and inspect residential properties (houses, apartments), commercial shops, residential plots, and agricultural land across major cities of Pakistan (such as Bhakkar, Lahore, Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Multan).
- **Property Owners and Sellers** can list properties with verified parameters (Marla/Kanal areas, PKR pricing, legal classification), manage inquiries, and track listing performance.
- **Administrators** ensure platform integrity by reviewing and approving listings, monitoring user activity, and moderating content.

---

## 2. Problem Statement & Market Analysis

In Pakistan, real estate accounts for significant economic wealth, yet digital real estate solutions frequently suffer from:
1. **Lack of Agricultural & Localized Land Representation:** Most platforms cater strictly to elite metropolitan housing schemes, ignoring secondary cities (like Bhakkar) and agricultural land (requiring tubewell, canal water share, and mouza tracking).
2. **Scattered and Misleading Listings:** Unverified listings with fake images, phantom prices, and unmanaged availability lead to buyer fatigue and wasted broker calls.
3. **Complex Communication Gaps:** Buyers lack an organized inquiry tracking history, and individual sellers struggle to filter serious buyers from casual inquiries.

---

## 3. Project Objectives

1. Develop a responsive, mobile-first web application connecting buyers, renters, and sellers.
2. Standardize property listings across 5 approved categories (Houses, Apartments, Commercial Shops, Residential Plots, Agricultural Land).
3. Implement secure authentication, password hashing with bcrypt, and Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
4. Build advanced multi-criteria search and filtering (City, Purpose, Category, Price Range, Area).
5. Implement direct inquiry lead management with role-specific dashboards.
6. Enforce rigorous Spec-Driven Development (SDD) principles ensuring full documentation traceability.
